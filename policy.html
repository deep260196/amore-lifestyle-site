Our Policies

Shipping

We ship across India. Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days and typically arrive within 3–5 business days, depending on your location.

Free shipping on orders above ₹999. Below that, a flat ₹79 shipping fee applies.

Returns & Exchanges

If something isn't right, you can request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

To start a return, message us on WhatsApp with your order details and we'll guide you through the next steps.

Payments

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, and net banking through Razorpay, a secure payment gateway. We never see or store your card details.

Privacy

We collect your name, contact details, and delivery address only to process and deliver your order. We don't sell or share your information with third parties.

Your bag and wishlist are saved on your own device and aren't visible to anyone else.

Questions?

Reach out anytime on WhatsApp or visit our Contact Us page.
